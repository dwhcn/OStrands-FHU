OStrands 0.8.0.2 lighting-compensation sliders
Based on https://gitlab.com/Keevan2374/ostrands at e3dcdff2f26e5f74dff304aa9cebea9183861b83.
OStrands by LeonVanWinkle (Saint), with contributions by Keevan2374 and crajjjj.
This modified build adds separately saved lighting compensation for strands and fluid.
The corresponding project source is included under docs.
Follow the included source's build.cmd with a current vcpkg and CommonLibSSE-NG v12.1.0.
The modified meshes can be regenerated with tools/make_strand.py and tools/make_fluid.py.
