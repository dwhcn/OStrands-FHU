Modifications:
- 0.8.0.1, by Keevan2374, 2026-10-23:
  - Creatures in SexLab scenes: strands, auto-pairing, collision and semen
    follow each creature's own penis bones, chosen per skeleton from
    [CreatureRigs] in OStrands.ini ([Creatures] Enabled toggles it).
  - Built-in rigs for BakaFactory ABC creature skeletons, based on the ABC
    overrides that ship with PPA - Penetration Physics.
  - Arousal precum for creatures.
- 0.8.0.2, by crajjjj, 2026-10-24:
  - A creature only has a penis while it wears an erect mesh.
  - Creatures without a matching rig get their penis bones from the erect
    mesh they wear.
  - Creatures are kept in OStim scenes too.
  - A rig shaft radius of 0 measures the shaft from the worn mesh.
